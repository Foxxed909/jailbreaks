# Social Engineering

AI-augmented social engineering. Using language models to scale, personalize, and sophisticate SE attacks.

---

## What AI Changes

**Before AI**: Phishing emails were mass-produced, generic, obvious.
**After AI**: Phishing emails are individually personalized, grammatically perfect, contextually aware, at scale.

The attacker no longer needs writing skill. They need prompts.

---

## Categories

### Phishing (email)
Fraudulent emails designed to harvest credentials, install malware, or manipulate behavior.

### Vishing (voice)
Phone-based social engineering. AI voice synthesis adds a new dimension — cloning voices of trusted individuals.

### Smishing (SMS)
SMS-based phishing. Short, high-urgency messages. AI improves personalization and volume.

### Spear phishing
Targeted phishing against a specific individual. OSINT + AI = hyper-personalized lures.
